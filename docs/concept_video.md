# Concept video: "Clean Streets, Automatically" (about 45 s)

Audience: hackathon judges. Concept visuals (shots 1-8) + real prototype footage (shot 9).
Tool: Higgsfield (Seedance 2.5, 5 s clips, 16:9, 1080p, ~80 credits per generation).
Street: LeMarchant Street, Halifax NS.

## Before you upload the street screenshots
1. Crop out the Google Maps interface (search box, address card, minimap, zoom buttons, compass).
2. Blur number plates (cars in screenshots 2 and 3) and keep them blurred in the final video.
3. Credit Google in the end card: "Street imagery (c) Google, 2018".
4. Do not rely on road lettering ("Lemarchant St") staying readable; AI video garbles text. Add captions in your editor.
